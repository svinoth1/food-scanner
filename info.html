📊 How Products Are Scored

Score = Health points − Additive points

Health points (Nutri-Score)

ABCDE
9075553515

No Nutri-Score? We use the NOVA processing group instead: 1 → 90 · 2 → 70 · 3 → 50 · 4 → 25.

Additive points

High −30Moderate −15Low −6None 0

Each additive counts once. The most taken away is 40, and the score never drops below 0.

Example

15
−40 additives

Nutri-Score C (55) with three Moderate dyes (−45, capped at −40) = 15.

What the score means

0–24 Bad25–49 Poor50–74 Good75+ Excellent

🔄 How Alternatives Are Picked

If a product scores below 90, we look for similar products that score higher.

Categories and sub-categories

Open Food Facts lists each product's categories from broad to specific:

ProductBroad → specific
NutellaBreakfasts → Spreads → Sweet spreads → Hazelnut spreads → Chocolate spreads → Cocoa and hazelnuts spreads
Coca-ColaBeverages → Non-alcoholic beverages → Carbonated drinks → Soft drinks → Sodas → Colas

How we search

1Colas
no better match? ↓
2Sodas
no better match? ↓
3Soft drinks
  1. Flip the list and try the last 3 categories, most specific first.
  2. Take the 40 most-scanned products there (by "unique scans") instead of hundreds.
  3. Score each one and keep the top 3 that beat your product.

So for Coca-Cola, you get another cola, not orange juice. Tap any alternative to see its full score.
